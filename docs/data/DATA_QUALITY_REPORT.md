## Caveat on near-duplicate detection

The validator flags a pair as a near-duplicate candidate if they share an exact `source_ref` OR
have >0.6 text similarity. This is **noisy for generic source_ref labels**: many oral-tradition
rows share the literal `source_ref` value `"Oral Tradition"` with no further pinpoint citation,
so they get flagged against each other even when the quotes are unrelated (e.g. #319 "We are
the ones we've been waiting for" vs #331 "One finger cannot lift a pebble" — both `Oral
Tradition`, nothing else in common). Roughly half the 45 flagged pairs are this false-positive
pattern. The other half — same specific citation (`Gita 2.47`, `Yasna 43:1`, several
`Dhammapada` verses, several Bahá'í `Gleanings` sections) — are more likely genuine variant
translations of the same verse and worth a human look.
