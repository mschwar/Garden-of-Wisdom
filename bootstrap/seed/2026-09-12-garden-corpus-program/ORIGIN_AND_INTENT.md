# Origin and intent

Garden of Wisdom should evolve from a curated quote collection into a provenance-aware, human-governed wisdom corpus.

The objective is not simply to accumulate more quotations. The system should separate discovery, intake, human curation, research, verification, enrichment, and canonical representation so automation can be broad upstream while canonical claims remain evidence-driven downstream.

## Core loops

1. **Acquisition loop** — discover or capture -> normalize -> detect likely duplicates -> human review.
2. **Truth loop** — investigate -> locate source -> compare wording -> establish attribution -> cite evidence -> record verification outcome -> enrich.

The operator owns the taste and admission decision. Evidence governs factual claims. Agents may search, infer, propose, and research; uncertainty must remain explicit until evidence resolves it.

## Critical distinction

Do not model the system as `intake -> approval -> final truth`.

Human approval means: **this item is worth pursuing and may belong in the Garden.** It does not mean: **the quotation and attribution are established as accurate.** Curation state and research state therefore need to remain separate.

## Classification intent

The desired ability to browse material across religious, literary, scientific, artistic, philosophical, cultural, oral, and other domains should be treated as a faceted-classification problem rather than a single mutually exclusive category field. Exact vocabularies should be learned from representative records before being hardened.
